01

The gap we address

Structural barriers prevent talented Black creatives from entering the UK screen industries — regardless of ability. We dismantle them.

02

How we deliver

Hands-on production and post-production training, one-to-one mentorship, and a sustained community pipeline. Real equipment, real crews, real films from day one.

03

Who we reach

Young creatives, career changers, migrants rebuilding UK credits — anyone from a Black or underrepresented background with the drive but not yet the access or confidence.

04

Measurable impact

Publicly screened films, verified UK production credits, improved employability and career confidence — and a growing pipeline into the professional screen industries.

Our Story

Built from
lived experience.

Made In Black Studios is a mission-led, community-centred organisation rooted in the belief that Black voices belong in every frame of UK film and television.

What We Do

Creating space.
Building futures.

We equip young people from Black, migrant and underrepresented communities with the skills, mentorship and pathways needed to build sustainable careers in the UK screen industries. We address the structural gap that talent alone cannot bridge.

◆ All surplus reinvested. No profit distribution. Ever.
Recognition & Reach
BBC◆ Deloitte◆ RTS◆ Grierson BAFTA-Nominated Projects◆ UK Global Talent

Led by award-winning filmmakers. Built on over 7 years of hands-on training in cinematography, lighting and colour grading. Supported by The Lazy Filmmaker education platform — empowering Black filmmakers worldwide.

Ibitayo Ibikunle

Founder & Creative Director

The Founder's Story

From Lagos to Manchester.
Built twice.

Ibitayo Ibikunle is a Nigerian filmmaker from Lagos who built a 10-year professional career before relocating to the UK in 2021 and rebuilding from the ground up — navigating an industry with no playbook for people who arrive without connections, capital, or anyone who looks like them in the room.

"I didn't just learn filmmaking. I learned how the industry works — and I realised most Black young people never get access to that knowledge at all."

His documentary Home Away — exploring migration, identity and belonging — earned international recognition and sits at the heart of why Made In Black Studios exists.

Flagship Programme

ShootYourShort

Train. Create. Screen.

A comprehensive production and post-production training experience for filmmakers interested in both scripted and unscripted film and television. From first contact with professional cinema-grade equipment to a publicly screened, finished film.

No prior experience required. No weekday commitment. No income threshold. The talent exists — what's missing is the room.

No experience required Saturdays only Open to all

No experience? That is not a barrier here.

ShootYourShort is designed for people who have not had the opportunity — not those who already have access. We particularly welcome applications from Black, Asian and other ethnically diverse backgrounds, migrants and internationally-trained creatives, people with caring responsibilities, those from low-income households, and anyone who has felt like the screen industries were not built for them.

This form takes around 10 minutes. There are no wrong answers. Be honest.
