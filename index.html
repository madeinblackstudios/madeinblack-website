Our Story

Built from
lived experience.

Made In Black Studios is a mission-led, community-centred organisation rooted in the belief that Black voices belong in every frame of UK film and television.

What We Do

Creating space.
Building futures.

We equip young people from Black, migrant and underrepresented communities with the skills, mentorship and pathways needed to build sustainable careers in the UK screen industries. We address the structural gap that talent alone cannot bridge.

◆ All surplus reinvested. No profit distribution. Ever.
Recognition & Reach
BBC◆ Deloitte◆ RTS◆ Grierson BAFTA-Nominated Projects◆ UK Global Talent

Led by award-winning filmmakers. Built on over 7 years of hands-on training in cinematography, lighting and colour grading. Supported by The Lazy Filmmaker education platform — empowering Black filmmakers worldwide.

Ibitayo Ibikunle

Founder & Creative Director

The Founder's Story

From Lagos to Manchester.
Built twice.

Ibitayo Ibikunle is a Nigerian filmmaker from Lagos who built a 10-year professional career before relocating to the UK in 2021 and rebuilding from the ground up — navigating an industry with no playbook for people who arrive without connections, capital, or anyone who looks like them in the room.

"I didn't just learn filmmaking. I learned how the industry works — and I realised most Black young people never get access to that knowledge at all."

His documentary Home Away — exploring migration, identity and belonging — earned international recognition and sits at the heart of why Made In Black Studios exists.
